TSA (Tsaraloha Structural Analysis) is a desktop application for modeling and analysing framed
structures. Most pages are written in French; a notice appears when the French version is shown.

> [!WARNING]
> TSA is under active development (version 0.1.0). Results must always be checked by a qualified
> structural engineer.
